# Deliverable Format (client-facing files)

Applies to every file in `01_deliverables/`.

## Header

```
{{deliverable_header in uppercase}}
# {{Document title}}
{{organization}} · {{framed question or metric · period}} · {{date}}
```

- `deliverable_header` comes from the profile settings (default: the organization name).
- When the header is "THE ANALYST'S PLAYBOOK", add the line
  "Move faster. Stand behind every number." under the title.

## Pre-read brief (pre-read-brief.md)

One page. Four sections, in this order, with these length limits:

1. **Finding** — 1–2 sentences. Names the metric, segment, time period, and size of change.
   No causes, no advice, no "because".
2. **Confidence** — 1–2 sentences. What is confirmed, what is directional, and what evidence
   would close the gap.
3. **Recommendation** — 1–3 sentences. A specific action, an owner, and a date. Nothing
   beyond what the evidence supports. Never "investigate X" without an owner and date.
4. **Rationale** — one short paragraph. The alternatives considered and why the evidence
   points away from them or leaves them open.

## Working hypothesis (working-hypothesis.md)

Label the title "Working hypothesis". Sections: Leading explanation · Why it is the strongest
candidate · What would confirm it (data, owner, time) · Alternatives still open ·
Low-risk next step now.

## Anticipated questions (anticipated-questions.md)

Numbered list. Each item: the question (bold), then a 2-sentence answer grounded in checks
already run, or an honest statement of what is unknown and how it will be found out.

## Action scope note (action-scope-note.md)

Proposed action · Evidence scope · Recommended targeted version · Trigger to scale up.

## Style

- Plain, specific, action-led. Short sentences. One idea per bullet.
- Use "confirmed" and "directional" in plain words; do not show bracketed evidence tags.
- Round figures sensibly and state record counts where they help credibility.
- No mention of AI, prompts, models, costs, checklists, depth, or tiers.
