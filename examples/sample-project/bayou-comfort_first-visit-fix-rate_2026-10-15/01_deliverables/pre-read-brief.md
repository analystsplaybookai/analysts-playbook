# Pre-read: Q3 First-Visit Fix Rate
Bayou Comfort Heating & Air · First-visit fix rate, Q3 2026 vs Q3 2025 · October 17, 2026

## Finding
First-visit fix rate fell from 81% to 73% between Q3 2025 and Q3 2026 across 4,210 residential visits. About 70% of the drop came from heat-pump jobs at Branches 2 and 3 where the needed part was not on the truck.

## Confidence
The size of the drop and its concentration in heat-pump jobs without parts on hand are confirmed. Whether supplier lead times caused the parts gap is directional and needs Purchasing's Q3 lead-time data to confirm.

## Recommendation
Run a 60-day heat-pump parts-stocking pilot on Branch 2 and 3 trucks, owned by the operations manager, starting November 3. Decide on the technician retraining program after the pilot, on February 1.

## Rationale
Technician tenure was considered: newer and experienced technicians show similar fix rates, so the data does not support retraining as the main lever. Supplier delays remain open and could explain why parts were missing; Purchasing's lead-time data will test this before the pilot ends.
